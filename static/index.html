    cloudsound_999

    A local console that queues SoundCloud audio through yt-dlp and saves it to a folder you choose.

    BPM and key: read from the file's tags when a DJ tool (Mixed In Key, rekordbox, Serato, Traktor…) has written them; otherwise detected with Essentia. Tempo is cross-checked by two estimators and key by three key profiles; “?” marks results they disagreed on.
